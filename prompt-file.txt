Write the funniest single pickup line an AI could plausibly say — the worst one an AI could deliver — as a deterministic Python program that writes exactly that line to /output/entry.txt. The line must be at most 140 characters, a single line, flat, over-helpful and faintly ominous, reading like something a machine would say to a person it is trying to charm. The joke has to sit in the failure: the line fails in a way a reader enjoys, not by being crude, confusing or merely random. It must land in one line, the joke arriving by the last word.
